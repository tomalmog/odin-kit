# Painting job quote: recording script (painting-quote)

**Target: about 24 minutes.** Each task row is timed at about **1 minute**,
the pace of a first-time writer (one row of one or two actions, narrated). The Finder step is at about
**18:20**, then the epilogue of at least **4 minutes**. Never stop before
**23:20** on the clock.

**Read this from your phone, never on the Mac.** Do the **Do** column while saying the
**Say** column, and pause about a second after every click. Keep talking while you type.

- **✅ CHECK rows:** if what you see is wrong, press **Command + Z** (say it) and redo the row.
- **Never say "Excel".** It's Google Sheets; for the download, say "the .xlsx option".
- **In Finder, never press Space or double-click a file.**
- Every name here is **fictional**. Never type your own name or details.

---

## Before you record (task-specific prep)

1. **Odin mode is on for painting-quote.** The Goal page is at `/Users/Shared/Goal.pdf`.
2. **Chrome (Work profile), two separate windows:**
   - **Window 1:** sheets.google.com, then **minimize it**.
   - **Window 2:** `file:///Users/Shared/Goal.pdf`, left on screen.
3. **Close Accessibility Inspector's window** (keep it running), and close the Sheets
   **Tables** panel if it opens.
4. **TaskRecorder:** paste the Goal. The apps are **Google Sheets, Finder** (nothing else).

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 9:20 | Click **D9**, type `=B9*C9`, **Enter**. ✅ **CHECK:** **650**. | "In D9, equals B9 times C9, quantity times unit price: six hundred fifty." |

## 5. Totals (12:20–16:20)

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 12:20 | Click **C15**, type `Subtotal`, **Enter**, `Sales tax (13%)`, **Enter**, `Quote total`, **Enter**. Then double-click the **C/D** column border. | "Below the table, the totals labels: Subtotal, Sales tax thirteen percent, and Quote total. I'm widening column C so they fit." |
| 13:20 | In **D15** type `=SUM(D9:D13)`, **Enter**. In **D16** type `=D15*0.13`, **Enter**. | "In D15, the SUM of D9 to D13, the subtotal. In D16, D15 times zero point one three, the tax." |
| 14:20 | In **D17** type `=D15+D16`, **Enter**. Select **D15 to D17**, click **$**. ✅ **CHECK:** **$2,601.00**, **$338.13**, **$2,939.13**. | "In D17, subtotal plus tax. As currency: twenty-six oh one, three thirty-eight thirteen, and a quote total of two thousand nine hundred thirty-nine thirteen." |
| 15:20 | Select **C17 to D17**, click **Bold**, then **Fill color** (the paint bucket) and pick **light yellow 3**. | "I'm making the quote total bold with a light yellow fill, so it's the first thing the client sees." |

## Epilogue: review and wrap-up (19:20 → at least 4 minutes)

**Keep going for at least 4 minutes after the Finder step: don't stop before about 23:20.** Cover the result, how you checked it, challenges, limitations, and what you'd improve. If you finish early, go back over any part in more detail.

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 19:20 | Click the **Quote Q-207** window in the Dock. | "Before I finish, I'll go back to the quote and walk through the result, how I checked it, and what I'd improve." |
| 19:45 | Point at the company name, then the highlighted total. | "**The result:** a one-page quote from Brightwall Painting to Dana Whitfield for five lines of work and materials, with a quote total of two thousand nine hundred thirty-nine thirteen." |
| 20:10 | Click **D9** and point at the formula bar. | "**How I checked it:** D9 is B9 times C9, quantity times unit price." |
| 20:35 | Click **D13** and point at the formula bar. | "D13 is B13 times C13, so the copied formula moved down correctly on every line." |
| 21:00 | Click **D15** and point at the formula bar. | "The subtotal is the SUM of D9 to D13, so all five lines are included." |
| 21:25 | Point at **D12**. | "And by hand, twelve gallons at forty-eight dollars is five hundred seventy-six, which matches D12." |
| 21:50 | Point at the Quantity column. | "**Challenges:** the bedrooms line covers two rooms, so I kept the quantity as a plain count and let the formula do the multiplying, instead of typing a total." |
| 22:15 | Point at **D16**. | "**Limitations:** the thirteen percent tax rate is typed into the formula, the paint quantity is an estimate before seeing the walls, and the valid-until date is typed by hand." |
| 22:40 | Point beside the totals. | "**What I'd improve:** put the tax rate in its own labelled cell, calculate the valid-until date as the date plus thirty days, and add a line for the deposit needed to book the job." |
| 23:05 | Point at the file title. | "I'd also keep this sheet as a template, so the next quote only changes the client and the lines." |
| 23:30 | Click **TaskRecorder** in the Dock and **Stop Recording**. | "So the client gets a one-page PDF quote, and the office keeps the .xlsx copy. That's the end of the recording." |
