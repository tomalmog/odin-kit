# Example: Painting job quote

The idea text, word for word. The recording must follow these steps in this order.

```
Domain / Role: Small business admin / home services. An office manager at a small painting company preparing a price quote for a homeowner.

Goal: Create a one-page price quote for painting three rooms in a fictional client's house, calculating each line's cost, the subtotal, 13% sales tax and the quote total, and produce a PDF to send to the client.

Starting materials: Starting from scratch with no input files. The company and client names are fictional, and the five quote lines (rooms and supplies with quantities and prices) are typed in during the recording.

Steps (with the apps for each):
1. Google Sheets: create a blank spreadsheet named "Quote Q-207".
2. Google Sheets: type the quote header (company name, client, quote number, date and "valid for 30 days") and make the company name bold and larger.
3. Google Sheets: type a table with Item, Quantity and Unit price for five lines (three rooms, paint and supplies), with bold headers.
4. Google Sheets: add a Cost column (Quantity times Unit price), copy it down, and format the prices and costs as currency.
5. Google Sheets: calculate the subtotal with SUM, 13% sales tax and the quote total, and make the total bold with a light fill colour.
6. Google Sheets: download the quote as a PDF and as an .xlsx file.
7. Finder: open the Downloads folder and confirm both files are there.

Deliverable: The Google Sheet "Quote Q-207" plus a one-page PDF and an .xlsx copy, with the quote header, five priced lines, subtotal, tax and quote total.
```

**Apps:** Google Sheets, Finder

**Final note / sources:** `No input files; all values typed during recording.`
